What I build

A suite of DJ tools on one shared engine: analysis, tagging, playback and broadcast, installed side by side on a single runtime. Key, BPM, energy and loudness detection built for harmonic mixing — not a plug-in for someone else's library, but the library itself.

How I work

Measured, not guessed

Numbers in my code come with the measurement that produced them, in a comment next to the line. When something surprising turns out to be true, the surprise is written down; when a claim of mine turns out to be wrong, the retraction stays in the file.

Protection in front of the action

There is no undo, by design. Anything that moves, renames or deletes your files says exactly what it is about to do, names every file it will touch, and waits. A move across drives copies, verifies byte for byte, and only then removes the original.

One engine, four faces

The apps share a core rather than resembling each other. When two of them disagree about something, that is a bug at the root — not a difference to be documented per app.

From DJs to DJs

Every feature is measured against what actually happens in a booth. A detector that scores well on a benchmark and fails on a track you are about to mix has not earned anything.
