HOW I WORK

Measured, derived, or chosen

Every number in my code is one of three things: measured, derived, or chosen — there is no fourth case. Claims keep the measurement that produced them, and when one turns out wrong, the retraction stays in the file.

The premise before the math

The expensive bug is not a wrong calculation — it is a correct calculation on a false premise, because nothing breaks. A surprising result gets checked against something external before it gets believed.

Protection in front of the action

There is no undo, by design. Anything that moves, renames or deletes your files says exactly what it is about to do, names every file it will touch, and waits.

The flaws are written down

Each project keeps a ledger of what is known to be broken and what was built on top of it anyway. A workaround that lives only in code gets explained away — and an empty list proves nothing.

Judged on the shipped thing

Verdicts come from the product — the built executable, the real screen — never from a flattering preview. A green test that proves nothing is a bug like any other.

AI behind a gate

Agents build for me around the clock, budgeted and scoped. Nothing they make is committed, shipped or believed until my own eyes and ears have signed it.
